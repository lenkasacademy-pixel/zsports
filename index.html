Reading the numbers. "Leads" are the campaign's optimisation event, offsite_conversion.fb_pixel_lead, on Meta's default attribution window. Placement, age and hour figures are separate breakdown queries and each sums to within ₹1 of the ₹1,082.08 campaign total; small gaps are Meta's rounding, not missing spend. Cost per lead is spend ÷ leads within each row, so rows with no leads show no cost per lead rather than a zero.

Caveats. One day, one ad set, still inside the learning phase — every split below the campaign level is a small sample, and the 55+, undeclared and Stories rows in particular are too thin to act on alone. Region-level lead counts were withheld by Meta's privacy thresholds and are not shown.
