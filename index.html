The morning that went wrong

9 September, hour by hour

Worth keeping on the record, because it is the only bad stretch in the five days and it explains why the blended number is higher than any recent day. A restart at 07:00 sent the campaign after very cheap, very low-quality traffic: ₹527.83 in a single hour bought 28,563 impressions and one lead. The campaign was rebuilt that afternoon.

The quiz is converting better than it did on day one

Store events, all traffic — not only visitors from ads

Two separate things decide whether an ad becomes a lead: how many people who land actually start the quiz, and how many who start it finish. Both are measured on the site itself, so they cover every visitor, not just the ones Meta attributes. Start rate has risen from 9.3% on day one to 13.6%; completion has held near 60% throughout.
